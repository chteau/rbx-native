Features / Renderer

Quality and calibration

Automatic quality scaling, quality bands and renderer calibration.

The viewport scales its quality level (1 to 21) to hold the frame rate, like Roblox. Set it yourself from the Viewport dock.

Settings

  • Studio Settings › Viewport › Advanced › Quality bands moves where each band ends.
  • Renderer calibration nudges the sun's base strength, atmosphere haze and Plastic's highlight for a place that looks off against Roblox. Each has a reset.
  • Low-memory GPUs step down until the place draws.

Compared to Studio

  • When the window loses focus the viewport throttles, and it only redraws when something changes, so it's light on a laptop.
