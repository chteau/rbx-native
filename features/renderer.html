Features / Renderer

Viewport and lighting

A native GPU viewport with Roblox's lighting, materials and sky.

The 3D view is our own renderer, written in Rust. The lighting model comes from Roblox's own shader sources and public docs, then checked side by side against Studio.

What it draws

  • All 45 materials with their textures, MaterialVariants and SurfaceAppearance (emissive too).
  • Shadows, point light shadows, bloom, colour correction, depth of field, blur, sun rays, atmosphere.
  • Sky boxes, the default sky, stars, night sky and clouds.
  • Parts, MeshParts, SpecialMeshes, trusses, decals and textures on real geometry, Glass refraction, ForceField.
  • GUIs: ScreenGuis, BillboardGuis and SurfaceGuis with full GuiObject support.

Camera

WASD and mouse look with eased motion, pan, orthographic mode from the Viewport dock, and an orientation indicator in the corner.

Limits

  • It's tuned to look like Roblox, not pixel-identical. Where Roblox publishes no constants, the numbers are ours.
