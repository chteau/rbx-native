Discord Rich Presence

Show what you're working on in your Discord profile.

Studio Settings › Account has a Discord Rich Presence toggle. It shows the open place, what you're editing (a script, the viewport or the UI Editor) and the elapsed time.

Good to know

  • Off by default.
  • A second toggle hides place and script names, for anything unannounced.
  • No Discord running means nothing happens.
