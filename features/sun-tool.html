Features / Editing and building

Sun tool and light guides

Point at the scene to place the sun, and see how far your lights reach.

The Sun tool on the Model page places the sun (or the moon, from its Moon tile) by pointing instead of typing a time and latitude.

How to use it

  • Sky: drag the sun across the sky.
  • Face: click a surface and the sun shines straight onto it.
  • Shadow: press an object and drag to where its shadow should fall.
  • Glint: click a surface and the sun moves so its reflection reaches the camera.
  • Each drag writes TimeOfDay and GeographicLatitude and is one undo step. Shadows follow live.

Light guides

Select a PointLight, SpotLight or SurfaceLight and the viewport draws its range, cone or frustum in the light's colour, like Studio's Show Light Guides. Toggle them in the Viewport dock.

Compared to Studio

  • Studio has no Sun tool, you'd type TimeOfDay and latitude by hand.
