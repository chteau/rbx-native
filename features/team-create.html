Features / Collaboration

Team Create with Studio

Work next to people in Studio, through the Team Presence plugin.

Native Team Create is Roblox's, so it's not something we can rebuild. Instead, a free Studio plugin connects people in Studio with people in RbxNative through a relay.

How to use it

  • People in Studio install the Team Presence plugin. RbxNative has its side built in.
  • Switch on Share my presence in the Collaborators panel (View › Collaborators in RbxNative).
  • Everyone opens the same place and is signed in to Roblox.

What you get

  • Each side sees the others' cameras, selections and open scripts.
  • Presence is end-to-end encrypted with the place's key, the relay only sees ciphertext.
  • A "while you were away" note lists changes since you last opened the place.
  • Add live co-editing to share edits too.

Limits

  • Presence needs someone with the place open in RbxNative to host the session. With only Studio users, the plugin waits.
  • Turning on Share my presence saves the place's key and relay on Roblox by itself.
