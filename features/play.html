Features / Play and testing

Play without Studio

Hit Play and the real Roblox client runs inside the editor.

Play publishes a copy of your place to a private sandbox experience, opens it in the Roblox client (Sober or Roséline on Linux, the Roblox app on macOS and Windows) and embeds the game window right over the 3D view.

How to use it

  • Hit Play on the Test tab. The first one takes a moment, the next ones start in about 4 seconds.
  • Click the game to give it the keyboard. Stop, closing Roblox or leaving the game ends the session.
  • Invite copies a link to the exact test server, so a friend can join your test.
  • Server and client prints, warnings and errors land in Output, tagged like Studio (Server blue, Client green). Click a Script:line to open it.
  • The Explorer and Properties can show the running game's tree for your client or the server, and edits there go to the game live.
  • The Test ribbon shows frame rate, ping and memory.

Compared to Studio

  • It's the real Roblox client running your game, so what you see is what players get.
  • Play follows a teleport instead of ending.

Limits

  • You need to be signed in to Roblox, and on Linux you need Sober or Roséline installed.
  • Places up to 150 MB.
  • If Sober can't join (like while it waits on an update), Play says so and uses Roséline if you have it.
